## ⚖️ Judging Criteria

Projects are evaluated on three criteria:

1. **Innovation & Creativity** — How original and imaginative is the idea? Does it solve a problem in a new or unique way?
2. **Technical Complexity** — How challenging was the implementation? Is technology being used effectively to demonstrate strong technical skills?
3. **Impact & Usefulness** — Does the project have real-world potential? How meaningful or helpful is it to users?

## 📜 Rules Summary

- All submissions must be **original work** built during the hackathon — pre-built or previously developed projects are not allowed
- **One submission per team** only
- No plagiarism — violations result in immediate disqualification
- Late submissions will not be considered
- Decisions of the judges are **final and binding**
- Teams must submit: source code (GitHub), project description, and demo video
- **MLH registration is compulsory** for all participants

By participating you agree to the [MLH Code of Conduct](https://static.mlh.io/docs/mlh-code-of-conduct.pdf) and [MLH Community Values](https://www.mlh.com/community-values).
