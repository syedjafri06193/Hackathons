## 📋 Rules Summary

- Open to participants **18 years and older** from **any country**
- Teams of **1–5 people**; teams are formed on [Devpost](https://devnetwork-ai-ml-hack-2026.devpost.com)
- Teams may build **any type of app** (mobile, web, business app) from scratch
- Teams may submit to **no challenges** (open build) or **as many sponsor challenges** as they wish
- **Two rounds of judging:**
  - **Round 1 (Overall):** Progress made, concept (does it solve a real problem?), feasibility (could it become a startup?)
  - **Round 2 (Sponsor):** Each sponsor judges submissions to their own challenge independently
- Teams **own their own IP**; ideas shared at the hackathon are released into the public domain — no NDAs required
- All sponsors are available to assist teams; contact info available on Devpost
